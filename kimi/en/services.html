Our Services

Complete and professional solutions for the maintenance of your trees and green spaces.

Home / Services

Tree pruning and trimming by climbing or aerial platform

Our pruning expertise allows us to work on all types of trees, regardless of their size or accessibility. We use two complementary techniques: tree climbing for narrow areas and aerial platform for large clearances.

Each pruning is carefully planned. We take into account the tree's biological characteristics, its immediate environment and your aesthetic expectations to propose the most suitable solution.

  • Training pruning for young trees
  • Maintenance pruning and dead wood removal
  • Crown reduction pruning
  • Cabling and securing

Fruit tree and shrub pruning

Fruit tree pruning is an art that requires knowledge and precision. We prune your fruit trees to optimize their production while preserving their health and longevity.

We also care for your ornamental shrubs and hedges to structure and beautify your garden throughout the year.

  • Fruit tree training pruning
  • Restructuring pruning
  • Hedge and shrub pruning
  • Planting advice

Specialized felling and stump removal

When a tree becomes dangerous or diseased, its felling must be carried out with the greatest precision. We master dismantling techniques by climbing, aerial platform and crane to intervene safely.

After felling, we offer complete stump grinding to free up space and prepare the ground for new plantings.

  • Technical felling in confined spaces
  • Dismantling by crane or aerial platform
  • Stump grinding
  • Green waste removal

Landscaping and outdoor design

Beyond trees, we design and implement the complete layout of your outdoor spaces. From wooden terraces to live hedges, we create gardens that reflect you.

We favor natural materials and local species for sustainable landscaping in harmony with the environment.

  • Wooden terrace design
  • Natural habitat creation
  • Live hedge planting
  • Wooden garden structures

Transport up to 4.5 tonnes

Our transport service complements our landscaping services. We deliver materials, soil, rubble and green waste up to 4.5 tonnes for your projects. This capacity allows us to ensure the autonomy of our sites and respect the deadlines agreed with our clients.

  • ✓ Material transport
  • ✓ Green waste removal
  • ✓ Site delivery
  • ✓ Up to 4.5 tonnes payload
